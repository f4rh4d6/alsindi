Field Methodology & Public Crowdsourcing

Unlike traditional architectural photography that waits for idealized empty moments, my field survey deliberately captures the physical frictions of the city: road barriers, municipal signs, maintenance crews, and the human presence that defines public architecture.

Through an active Instagram community of over 48,000 followers, coordinates are submitted directly by residents living in post-war estates, industrial transit corridors, and rapidly gentrifying districts. Each survey trip begins with these crowdsourced field notes.
